What are network, voice, and patient engagement services?

Network, voice, and patient engagement services cover the connectivity and communication backbone of a healthcare organization: the network, the phone and messaging systems that run on it, and the platforms that reach patients. ITSN designs and runs that backbone as a single, optimized system for healthcare, health tech, and manufacturing organizations.

It’s for IT and operations leaders dealing with unreliable connectivity, ageing phone systems, or patient communication tools that don’t connect to anything else.

How do we fix dropped calls, dead zones, and unreliable connectivity?

These problems usually come from disconnected networks and bolt-on tools that were never designed to work together. We assess the network as a whole, then redesign it for resilience and coverage where your staff and patients actually are.

After deployment, monitoring and ongoing optimization keep it that way. The result is fewer outages and smoother experiences for the people who depend on the network.

How do we modernize a hospital phone system without replacing the whole network?

You don’t need a rip-and-replace project. We integrate with what works and modernize in stages to avoid disruption, moving to unified communications and modern voice at a pace your operations can absorb.

For clinical teams, that can include clinical voice and documentation tools, selected and integrated so they work with your network and core systems.

How do we make it easier for patients to reach us?

Patients expect to reach you through the channel that suits them. We implement messaging and patient communication platforms, and connect them to your core systems so communication flows without manual rekeying. Examples include platforms like Artera and Infobip.

The aim is better access for patients and clearer collaboration for your teams, not another standalone tool.

How do we reduce telecom and connectivity costs?

Costs build up when circuits, contracts, and platforms are bought one at a time. We right-size connectivity to what you actually use and consolidate contracts across providers. This typically reduces spend within the first optimization cycle.

Because we’re vendor-neutral, platforms are chosen because they fit your environment, not because of our margin. As a Value Added Reseller (VAR), we can also manage procurement and renewals.

How does network design support security?

The network is also your first line of containment. A well-designed network can be segmented, so that an incident in one area doesn’t take down everything else. Our security practice works alongside the network team on zero-trust and segmentation design.

For the practical steps, see our article on healthcare network security best practices.
