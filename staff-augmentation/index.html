What is IT staff augmentation?

IT staff augmentation adds vetted specialists to your existing team for a defined need, without the time and cost of a permanent hire. ITSN provides IT, cloud, and security specialists who work inside your processes and tools, for healthcare, health tech, and manufacturing organizations.

It suits IT leaders who need a specific skill for a project, a critical role covered quickly, or extra capacity during a transformation. Your team directs the work. Our people work to your goals and your standards.

How do we fill a critical IT or security role quickly?

Hiring full-time for every skill gap is slow and expensive, and a vacant security or infrastructure role carries risk every week it stays open. Because we maintain an existing bench, most placements move in days to a couple of weeks, not months.

Timelines depend on the role. We’ll tell you at the start what is realistic for the skills you need.

How do we add capacity for a migration or project without hiring full-time?

Projects such as cloud migrations, network refreshes, and security programs need skills for a period, not forever. Augmentation lets you scale capacity up or down by project phase, then release it when the work is done.

Engagements can take several forms: a bucket of hours, a fractional specialist, a project team, or a full-time placement. Many clients start with one model and move to another as their needs change.

Will augmented staff understand a regulated environment?

Generic contractors rarely understand healthcare or manufacturing environments. Our specialists are fluent in regulated settings, which means less ramp-up and fewer surprises. Before they start delivering, they onboard to your processes and security standards.

They can work on-site, remotely, or in a hybrid model. We match the working model to your security requirements and how your team is set up.

What happens if we need more than one specialist can provide?

Every placement is backed by our wider consulting and engineering bench. If a question goes beyond one person’s expertise, the specialist can draw on our consulting, security, and Voice & Data teams.

That backing is the difference between a contractor and an extension of your team. You get the individual you need, with the wider team’s experience behind them.

Which roles does ITSN commonly fill?

  • Cloud and infrastructure engineers for migrations, refreshes, and day-to-day operations.
  • Security and compliance specialists for assessments, monitoring, and audit preparation.
  • Network and connectivity engineers for design, segmentation, and optimization.
  • Solution architects to design systems that fit together.
  • Project and transformation leads to keep complex programs on track.
  • Service desk and support staff to keep users productive.
