What are healthcare managed security services?

Healthcare managed security services are an outsourced security operation for organizations that handle patient data. A partner monitors your environment, detects and investigates threats, leads the response, and keeps the controls and evidence HIPAA expects. ITSN delivers them vendor-neutral, building around the security tools you already own wherever they are effective.

MSSP, MDR or in-house SOC: which do we need?

A managed security service provider (MSSP) runs and monitors a broad set of security controls for you. Managed detection and response (MDR) focuses on finding and stopping active threats, with hands-on response. An in-house security operations center gives the most control but needs analysts on every shift, which is out of reach for most mid-market healthcare organizations.

Many choose a managed service, or a hybrid in which our specialists work alongside your team through security staff augmentation.

How does managed security support HIPAA compliance?

The HIPAA Security Rule requires risk analysis, risk management, audit controls and security incident procedures. A managed security service supplies the monitoring, logs, incident records and reports that show those safeguards are working. A provider that can access ePHI is a business associate and signs a business associate agreement (BAA).

Can you work with our existing security tools?

Yes. ITSN is vendor-neutral. We assess the tools you already own, keep the ones that are effective, and fill gaps with best-fit technology from partners such as Cisco, so you are not forced into a rip-and-replace.

Where should we start?

Start with a HIPAA security risk assessment. It shows where ePHI is exposed and which risks matter most, so monitoring and response focus on the systems that would hurt patient care or compliance if they failed.
