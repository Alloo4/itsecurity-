About IT Security Now

A trusted technology partner for healthcare organizations.

Vendor-neutral IT consulting, security, and technology guidance for mid-market and enterprise healthcare, health tech, and manufacturing.

IT Security Now (ITSN) is a vendor-neutral IT consulting, security, and Value Added Reseller (VAR) firm based in Tampa, Florida. We serve mid-market and enterprise healthcare, health tech, and manufacturing organizations.

Our founder, Dan Drees, started ITSN after seeing technology and security decisions repeatedly slow progress instead of advancing it. We exist to help organizations make smarter, more defensible technology investments, without disruption or uncertainty.

We work as an extension of your IT leadership team, delivering strategic guidance, procurement expertise, implementation support, and lifecycle management that drive measurable business outcomes.

◆ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is IT Security Now (ITSN)?

IT Security Now (ITSN) is a vendor-neutral IT consulting, security, and Value Added Reseller (VAR) firm based in Tampa, Florida. We serve mid-market and enterprise healthcare, health tech, and manufacturing organizations.

What size organizations does ITSN work with?

Healthcare organizations with 500 to 2,000 employees often see significant value, because they need enterprise-grade technology expertise but may not have large specialized teams in-house. Healthcare systems, physician groups, behavioral health organizations, ambulatory care networks, and post-acute providers can all benefit.

Does ITSN help with HIPAA and compliance initiatives?

We are not a legal compliance authority, but we help implement technologies and security controls that support HIPAA, HITECH, and other healthcare compliance requirements. That includes data protection, audit logging, identity management, encryption, backup and disaster recovery, access controls, and security monitoring.

Will working with ITSN increase our technology costs?

In many cases we help organizations reduce costs by identifying redundant technologies, optimizing licensing agreements, consolidating vendors, preventing costly implementation mistakes, and improving technology utilization. The objective is not simply to purchase technology, but to maximize return on investment.

How quickly can ITSN begin delivering value?

Many organizations see value immediately through initial assessments, architecture reviews, security evaluations, and vendor consolidation discussions. Longer-term benefits typically include improved security posture, greater operational efficiency, and reduced technology complexity.
