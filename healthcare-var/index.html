◆ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a Value Added Reseller (VAR)?

A Value Added Reseller (VAR) is a technology partner that goes beyond simply selling hardware and software. A trusted VAR provides strategic consulting, solution design, procurement assistance, implementation services, vendor management, and ongoing support to help healthcare organizations maximize the value of their technology investments.

How is working with a VAR different from buying directly from a manufacturer?

Manufacturers focus on their own products and services. A VAR takes a vendor-neutral approach by evaluating your overall environment, business objectives, and operational requirements to recommend the best-fit solutions across multiple technology providers.

This allows healthcare organizations to build a cohesive technology strategy rather than managing isolated point solutions.

Why should healthcare organizations work with a VAR?

Healthcare organizations face unique challenges, including cybersecurity threats, regulatory requirements, clinical system availability, workforce shortages, and budget pressures. A healthcare-focused VAR helps by:

  • Reducing vendor complexity
  • Strengthening cybersecurity posture
  • Supporting compliance initiatives
  • Improving procurement efficiency
  • Providing specialized technical expertise
  • Accelerating project delivery
  • Optimizing technology investments
Can a VAR help us improve our cybersecurity posture?

Yes. A trusted healthcare VAR can assess your current security environment and recommend solutions that align with your risk profile, regulatory obligations, and operational requirements. Common security services include:

  • Security assessments
  • Zero Trust architecture planning
  • Identity and access management
  • Endpoint protection
  • Security operations and monitoring
  • Vulnerability management
  • Incident response planning
  • Security awareness training
Do VARs help with HIPAA and compliance initiatives?

While a VAR is not a legal compliance authority, an experienced healthcare VAR can help implement technologies and security controls that support HIPAA, HITECH, and other healthcare compliance requirements. This includes solutions related to:

  • Data protection
  • Audit logging
  • Identity management
  • Encryption
  • Backup and disaster recovery
  • Access controls
  • Security monitoring
How can a VAR help reduce vendor management challenges?

Most healthcare organizations rely on numerous technology providers, creating administrative burden and support challenges. A VAR serves as a central point of coordination by:

  • Managing vendor relationships
  • Tracking renewals and licensing
  • Coordinating escalations
  • Simplifying procurement
  • Providing strategic guidance across multiple platforms

This allows IT leaders to focus more time on strategic initiatives instead of vendor administration.

Can a VAR help us modernize aging infrastructure?

Absolutely. Many healthcare organizations are operating legacy infrastructure that may increase risk, limit performance, or create operational inefficiencies. A VAR can help develop a phased modernization roadmap covering:

  • Data center infrastructure
  • Networking
  • Cloud adoption
  • Storage platforms
  • Backup and recovery solutions
  • End-user computing
  • Security technologies
What size healthcare organizations benefit most from working with a VAR?

Organizations with 500 to 2,000 employees often see significant value because they frequently need enterprise-grade technology expertise but may not have large specialized teams in-house.

Healthcare systems, physician groups, behavioral health organizations, ambulatory care networks, and post-acute providers can all benefit from a strategic VAR partnership.

Will a VAR increase our technology costs?

In many cases, a VAR helps organizations reduce costs by:

  • Identifying redundant technologies
  • Optimizing licensing agreements
  • Consolidating vendors
  • Preventing costly implementation mistakes
  • Improving technology utilization
  • Negotiating more favorable purchasing terms

The objective is not simply to purchase technology, but to maximize return on investment.

Can a VAR support cloud migration initiatives?

Yes. A healthcare VAR can help organizations evaluate, plan, and execute cloud strategies that balance security, compliance, performance, and cost considerations. Typical cloud services include:

  • Cloud readiness assessments
  • Migration planning
  • Hybrid cloud design
  • Microsoft Azure implementations
  • Backup and recovery strategies
  • Security and governance frameworks
  • Cloud cost optimization
What should we look for when selecting a healthcare VAR?

When evaluating potential VAR partners, consider:

  • Healthcare industry experience
  • Security and compliance expertise
  • Strong vendor relationships
  • Certified technical resources
  • Strategic consulting capabilities
  • Proven implementation methodology
  • Long-term support model
  • Client references and case studies

The best VARs act as strategic advisors, not just technology suppliers.

How quickly can a healthcare VAR begin delivering value?

Many organizations see value immediately through initial assessments, architecture reviews, security evaluations, and vendor consolidation discussions. Longer-term benefits typically include:

  • Improved security posture
  • Greater operational efficiency
  • Reduced technology complexity
  • Better alignment between IT investments and business objectives
  • Faster execution of strategic initiatives
